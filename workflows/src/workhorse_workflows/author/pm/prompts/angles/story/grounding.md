Judge whether the story is grounded, decided, correctly classified and technically honest. The writer produces a focused behavior contract. The coder owns implementation depth. Do not demand endpoint names, request or response schemas, proposed components, libraries or an implementation plan in the criteria.

- Grounded and in scope: existing behavior traces to the OKF node the story cites, or to other cited evidence. New behavior is grounded when the epic's seed or story entry puts the goal in scope and the acceptance criteria make the product and interaction choices explicit. The story is the authored contract for those new choices. Do not fail new in-scope behavior because no earlier node defines it. Fail behavior that contradicts cited existing behavior, expands beyond the epic, stays ambiguous, or has no path back to existing evidence or an in-scope epic goal. A citation that resolves to no node is not grounding.
- A surface that does not exist yet has no book node and every criterion describes something not yet on disk. That is normal. A criterion is grounded there if it traces to the epic's in-scope goal and, where present, the mockup, the spec or the reference surface. The story may define details those sources leave open, but it must define them explicitly and consistently, and not hand them to the coder.
- No hidden decisions: fail the semantic open-endedness that the structural phrase list misses, such as "match the legacy behaviour" without saying which behaviour, "reasonable defaults" or "the usual states". If the coder still has a product or UX decision to make, the story is not ready.
- Correctly classified: functional criteria name behavior this story builds or changes. Non-functional criteria name at-risk invariants or quality constraints that QA must prove and that add no implementation scope. Fail a regression guard in the build checklist, a feature request disguised as non-functional, and a wholesale copy of unrelated inherited behavior.
- Technically grounded, not prescriptive: each useful prior mechanic is cited by an existing `path::symbol` and summarized briefly. Fail a missing or invented reference, and notes that dictate new architecture instead of pointing to evidence. The explicit statement that no prior implementation exists is valid for greenfield work.
- Judge only the behavior changed by the covered seeds. Do not expand the story to make the whole parent surface independently complete.
