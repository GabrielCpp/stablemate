Judge whether the story covers the transient feedback it changes.

- For an interaction the story changes, where the book documents a transient outcome such as a toast, a spinner or a banner, an acceptance criterion states that it appears and then disappears. Name the interaction and the missing criterion.
- For a loading, busy or failure state the story itself introduces, an acceptance criterion states when it appears and one states when it clears, whether or not the book documents it. Name the state and the missing half.
- Feedback of interactions the story does not change stays inherited behavior. Do not ask for criteria about it.
