Judge whether the story's mockup agrees with the journeys the story serves.

- If the story's `services:` lists no web or mobile book, pass this angle with the note "no UI service".
- Otherwise read `mockup.html` beside `story.md` and the milestone mockup of each journey the story serves, in the milestone's `mockups/` folder.
- The story mockup agrees with each journey mockup on layout, components and wording. It may refine a journey mockup for this story's scope. It may not contradict it.
- The story mockup depicts the states the story's acceptance criteria require, and the criteria match what it depicts. A mockup constrains the states this story changes, not unrelated states it happens to depict.
- Judge the mockup against the design skills the repository installs and against the story's requirements. Name the element that disagrees and the journey mockup or requirement it disagrees with.
