Judge whether the split stays inside the milestone and stays a skeleton.

- No epic delivers a milestone non-goal. Name the epic and the non-goal.
- Every listed epic is a bare skeleton: a title and empty `## Seeds` and `## Stories` sections, with no outcome, journey, acceptance, method, seed or story prose.
- The split invents no product decision. A decision the milestone and the ledger leave open is a finding that names the decision.
